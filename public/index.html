Before anything happens, someone should know.

This is a conversation, not a form. It collects the practical things your family would need to know if something happened to you — contacts, accounts, processes, where things are — so nothing is lost to the person who could have explained it.

This isn't a will, and it won't give you legal, financial, tax, or medical advice. It only helps you record what you know, and flags what you don't. Nothing is turned into a finished document here — that happens later, from what you record.
